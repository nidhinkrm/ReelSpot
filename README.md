# 📍 ReelSpot

### Don't just save the Reel. Visit the place.

Ever saved an Instagram Reel showing an amazing cafe, a must-try food spot, or a beautiful travel destination, only to forget about it later?

I've done this too. We discover interesting places every day, save them on Instagram, share them with friends, and eventually forget they even exist. Sometimes, we might even visit the same area without realizing that a place we'd saved is nearby.

**That's the problem I'm trying to solve with ReelSpot.**

ReelSpot is an Android app that helps turn places discovered through social media into real-world experiences. Save interesting locations, organize them on a map, and get reminded when you're nearby.

The idea is simple: save less for someday, and explore more today.

---

## ✨ What can ReelSpot do?

- 📍 **Save places** — Keep interesting destinations in one place instead of digging through saved posts and old chats.
- 🗺️ **Explore on a map** — See your saved places and where they're located.
- 🎬 **Keep Reel links** — Store the original Instagram Reel link alongside a place.
- 🔔 **Nearby reminders** — Get notified when you're close to a saved location, with the required permissions enabled.
- 🏷️ **Categories and tags** — Organize places by what they are and what makes them interesting.
- 📝 **Add your own notes** — Keep useful details about places you want to visit.

*Features may vary depending on the current development version.*

## 💡 Why I started this

This project started with a simple problem discussed with a friend: we often save Reels of places we'd love to visit, but forget about them when we actually get the chance.

That conversation got me thinking: what if saved places could appear on a map, and the app could remind us when we're nearby?

Instead of building another social media app, I wanted to build something that helps people actually experience the places they discover online.

ReelSpot is my attempt to turn that idea into something useful.

## 🚀 What's next?

ReelSpot is still a work in progress, and there are plenty of things I'd like to explore:

- 🤖 **AI-powered location suggestions** from Reel captions and available text.
- 🌐 **Better language support**, including Malayalam and mixed Malayalam-English content.
- 🔥 **Trending near me** — Discover popular places based on community activity.
- 💾 **Data backup and restore** — Keep saved places safe and make them easier to transfer.
- ⚙️ **More user controls** — Settings for nearby alerts, permissions, and app preferences.

These are planned improvements, not promises that every feature is already available.

## 📱 Getting started

To try ReelSpot:

1. Download the latest APK from the project's Releases page, when available.
2. Install it on your Android device.
3. Grant location and notification permissions if you want to use nearby reminders.
4. Start saving places you'd like to visit.

If you're installing an APK directly, Android may ask you to allow installation from that source.
